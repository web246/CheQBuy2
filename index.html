GOOD QUESTIONS

Trade is easier
when it’s clear.

Here are a few things people ask before getting started. Still wondering? Our team can help.

Ask us directly →
What does CheQBuy help with?

We bring product and supplier discovery, air and sea shipping options, shipment updates, and practical trade support together. Tell us what you’re trying to do and we’ll help identify the next step.

Can you help me find a product or supplier?

Yes. Start with the product you need and any details you already have. CheQBuy can help you explore product and supplier options through our platform and team.

What shipping options are available?

CheQBuy supports air and sea freight options. Available routes, rates and delivery arrangements depend on your goods and destination, so contact us for details on your shipment.

Can I track a shipment in the app?

The CheQBuy app includes shipment-tracking tools and shipping-rate information. Sign in or contact the team if you need help with a particular order.

Does CheQBuy handle customs and paperwork?

Our team can provide guidance with customs and trade paperwork as part of your shipment support. Requirements vary by goods and destination, so we’ll help clarify what applies to your case.

How do I get started or request a quote?

Message our team with what you need, where it’s going, and any product or shipment details you have. We’ll follow up to understand your request and discuss the available options.
