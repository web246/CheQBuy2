CheQBuy African Trade - static marketing site and storefront
Open index.html for the brand landing page or webapp.html for the product storefront.
The storefront opens to a marketplace home with category browsing, featured products and
search. Its bottom navigation links to Home, Shop, Saved, Cart and Orders. The catalog
supports product search and category filters, with a browser-saved cart and wishlist.
Sign-in and order placement are handed off to the existing CheQBuy web app. The preview
cart is not transferred to that app; customers must add those products again after signing in.
The marketing page includes a labeled, keyboard-accessible 3D app-screen carousel.
App screen previews are cropped to hide the phone status and system navigation bars.
The app-download badges use store search pages until direct listing links are available.
